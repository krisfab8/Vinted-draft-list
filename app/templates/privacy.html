Privacy.

What's stored

Your item photos, the listings made from them, prices, sales you record, and your name, email and app preferences.

Where

On the app's server and in a private cloud backup (Backblaze B2), so drafts survive restarts. Your phone also keeps a backup copy in this browser.

Who sees it

Only you. Each account's listings, photos and sales are kept separate; the person who invited you can see how many listings you've made this month, not what they are. Photos are sent to an AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) just to read labels and write the listing. Nothing is sold or used for ads.

Your password

Stored only as a secure scrambled code (a hash), never as the password itself. Your Vinted and eBay logins stay in the phone app, not on our server.

Deleting it

Settings → Delete my data removes every listing, photo, sale and your profile, from the server, the cloud backup and this phone.
