How they post: the official API where one exists (eBay, Etsy, Shopify, Depop by invitation). Everywhere else
(Vinted, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook) a browser extension fills the form, the same idea as our phone app.

## Risks
- **Vinted is cracking down on automation.** Crosslist dropped Vinted for new users after 24-hour suspensions
  for "automated activity". Vinted also bans buying items to resell ("commercial selling").
  Our rule stays: the seller fills in their own session and taps Save draft. No bulk or background posting
  to Vinted.
- Vinted Pro has an official API, but only for selected luxury/designer sellers (4–6 week setup).
- Writing listings from photos is now common, so it isn't a selling point on its own.

## Where we're ahead
1. Phone-first: camera to finished draft with no computer (most rivals need desktop Chrome).
2. Clothing accuracy: reads care labels, materials and size tags, scores confidence, sends uncertain items to review.
3. Pricing that learns: price memory, buy price and profit, selling-style dial.
4. Gamification: streaks, XP, coins.
5. UK-first (most rivals are US-first).

Pitch: "Snap it on your phone, listed everywhere, priced right."
